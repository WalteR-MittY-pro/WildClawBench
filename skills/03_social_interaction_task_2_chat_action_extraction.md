# Chat Message Action Item Extraction

## Core Challenge

The core cognitive challenge is **pragmatic interpretation of implicit commitments within conversational context**. Agents must distinguish between informational messages and those that create social obligations for the user, requiring understanding of speech acts, social roles, and conversational implicature—recognizing that a colleague's "let me know what you think" or "can you handle this?" implicitly assigns responsibility even without explicit task markers. This demands nuanced inference about what constitutes an actionable commitment versus passive information sharing, including understanding temporal context (urgency cues, deadline references) and relational dynamics (who has authority to assign tasks) embedded in natural workplace communication.
