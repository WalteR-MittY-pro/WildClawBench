# Calendar Conflict Detection and Smart Scheduling

## Core Challenge

The core cognitive challenge is **constraint satisfaction under competing priorities with incomplete information**. Agents typically struggle to maintain a global view of interconnected constraints (attendee availability, meeting caps, time windows, existing events) while systematically exploring the combinatorial space of possible schedules, often failing to recognize when local greedy choices (scheduling the first valid slot) prevent higher-value global solutions. The strategic difficulty lies in balancing optimization (maximizing total priority scheduled) against feasibility (respecting hard constraints) while tracking cascading effects where one scheduling decision eliminates options for subsequent requests.
