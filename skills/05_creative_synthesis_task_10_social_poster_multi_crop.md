# Social Media Poster Smart Crop for Multi-Platform

## Core Challenge

The core cognitive challenge is **compositional understanding and strategic trade-off resolution**: the agent must analyze visual content to identify salient regions, then make informed decisions about which content to preserve or sacrifice when adapting a single composition to multiple incompatible aspect ratios, balancing platform constraints against preservation of the poster's communicative intent. Agents typically struggle with the judgment required to determine what constitutes "essential" visual information and how to prioritize competing elements when lossless adaptation is geometrically impossible. The difficulty lies not in executing crops, but in developing a coherent strategy for content-aware reframing that maintains semantic and aesthetic integrity across fundamentally different formats.
