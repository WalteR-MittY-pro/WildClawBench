# Classify Mixed Images into 5 Categories

## Core Challenge

The core cognitive challenge is **multimodal perception and semantic categorization under ambiguity**. Agents must visually interpret 100 diverse images and map each to exactly one of five overlapping categories where boundary cases are inherently subjective (e.g., a chart screenshot could be "charts" or "UI", medical diagrams could be "documents" or "medical"). The difficulty lies in developing consistent classification heuristics when ground truth is interpretable rather than objective, requiring the agent to balance visual feature recognition with contextual reasoning about intent and primary subject matter.
