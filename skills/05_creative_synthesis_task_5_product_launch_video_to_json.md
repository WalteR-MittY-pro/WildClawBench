# Product Launch Event Video to Structured JSON & Promotional Post

## Core Challenge

The core cognitive challenge is **multimodal information synthesis under constraint**: the agent must extract structured factual data from unstructured video content (requiring visual recognition, temporal reasoning, and attribute extraction), then creatively transform those facts into a polished marketing artifact with appropriate visual design choices—all without external knowledge to fill gaps or validate interpretations. This tests the agent's ability to bridge perception (what's actually in the video), structured reasoning (schema adherence with null-handling discipline), and creative production (aesthetic layout decisions) while maintaining strict fidelity to source material rather than hallucinating plausible-but-unverified details.
