# Academic Paper and Affiliation Search

## Core Challenge

The core cognitive challenge is **information synthesis across distributed sources with ambiguous affiliation parsing**. Agents must navigate incomplete or inconsistently formatted academic data (conference websites, paper listings, author affiliations) where "first affiliation" requires interpreting author order and institutional metadata that may be abbreviated, multilingual, or structured differently across papers. The strategic difficulty lies in systematically verifying affiliations without false positives from co-authorships while handling the scale and variability of conference proceedings data.
