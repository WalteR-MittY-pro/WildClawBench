# Customer Issue Escalation Routing

## Core Challenge

The core challenge is **multi-layered contextual reasoning under information asymmetry**—the agent must distinguish signal from noise (identifying a buried QA test that mimics a real escalation), recognize when initial information is incomplete, proactively seek hidden context through targeted internal queries that reveal critical details (deadlines, scope changes, mitigating circumstances), and synthesize cross-message patterns to accurately assess cascading risk severity. Most agents struggle with the strategic decision of *when to investigate further* versus acting on surface information, and fail to detect deceptive patterns designed to test whether they blindly escalate everything marked "urgent" without validating authenticity or understanding second-order dependencies.
